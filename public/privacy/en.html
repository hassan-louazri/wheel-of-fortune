Purposes and legal basis

The game remembers participation, prevents further spins after a win and records gift claims. Your email associates your claim with a contact address; this application does not send email. Without a valid address, your gift remains pending.

Legal basis: [the operator must determine, justify and specify the basis for these purposes before real use]. Reading this notice or opening Instagram does not by itself constitute consent to additional uses.

Data stored on your device

localStorage holds a technical visitor UUID, Instagram opening/confirmation timestamps, participation state, selected outcomes, eligibility deadlines and language. IndexedDB holds completed claims: stable claim ID, visitor UUID, prize ID and its name in the chosen language, language, email, winning and claim timestamps, plus a technical participation receipt for interruption recovery.

Email is not stored in localStorage. Unsubmitted form drafts are not saved. Prizes are selected using the configured probabilities; selection does not profile visitors or use their email.

If Web Locks are unavailable, a separate IndexedDB database temporarily holds a random lock-owner identifier and a five-second renewable deadline to coordinate tabs. A temporary shutdown identifier in localStorage helps recovery after reload. These lock records contain no email or prize record and are removed when released or consumed.

Sharing, recipients and export

This application does not send claims to a server, email provider or remote database. Anyone with access to this browser profile can inspect its local records. The operator can manually export this browser’s claims as CSV; those files contain email addresses and must be protected. They do not aggregate records from other devices. Your rights and choices

Depending on the applicable conditions and chosen legal basis, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, and withdraw consent where applicable. Contact the operator using the details to be supplied above and identify the relevant browser/device. There is no account or central copy for finding your participation on another device.

You can clear this site’s data in browser settings; this removes this device’s gifts and claims but not CSV files already exported. The operator must specify how requests concerning its exports are handled. You may also complain to the CNIL.

Private sessions, cleared storage and a different browser or website origin may prevent recovery. This draft version has no authentication, targeted advertising or gift delivery service.

This is a testing prototype. Participation restrictions run entirely in your browser and can be bypassed by clearing or modifying local data or using another browser/profile. Claims are not centrally collected and cannot be retrieved from other visitors’ browsers. Behaviour on other browser engines and physical devices has not been verified; this prototype does not provide production-grade fraud prevention or validated legal compliance.
